## Map

Gujarat and neighbouring state outlines: Natural Earth 1:10m admin-1 boundaries, public domain
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). Simplified by `scripts/build-map-geometry.mjs`.
Rivers, roads, distances and landmark glyphs are hand-drawn illustration and approximate.
